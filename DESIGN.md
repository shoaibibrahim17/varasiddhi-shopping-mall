# Design System: Shri Varasiddhi Shopping Mall

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Celebration Folio"**

The visual world borrows from the composure of a fashion lookbook and the sense of occasion in an Indian invitation folio: deliberate chapters, textile-rich imagery, fine rules, strong cropping, and moments of saturated colour held inside a restrained neutral field. It refuses the category-default marketplace grid and the generic centred local-business hero.

The page should feel art-directed rather than decorated. Campaign artwork may appear intact when it is the subject, while model-led crops create original page compositions without altering source assets. Repetition is controlled through changes in scale, density, alignment, and background field.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Editorial asymmetry with a clear reading order.
- Warm paper grounds, deep textile colour fields, and rare gold accents.
- Strong image crops alongside intact campaign plates.
- Hairline rules, folio numbers, and compact labels instead of ornamental UI chrome.
- Flat, composed surfaces with almost no conventional card treatment.

## Colors

Use a restrained strategy: warm neutrals carry the majority of the page, espresso anchors typography, and gold appears only as a precise accent. Asset-derived crimson, indigo, and maroon may own occasional full sections when the featured garment justifies them.

**The Gold Is Punctuation Rule.** Gold marks rules, labels, and small interaction details; it does not become the page background.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bodoni Moda Variable with Georgia and serif fallbacks.

**Body Font:** Instrument Sans Variable with system sans-serif fallbacks.

**Character:** Bodoni creates selective high-fashion contrast in large titles and occasional italics. Instrument Sans carries navigation, body copy, captions, and interaction labels with contemporary clarity.

**The Selective Bodoni Rule.** Display typography is reserved for editorial hierarchy; utility copy and long reading stay in Instrument Sans.

## Layout

The system uses mobile-first full-bleed bands, a consistent content gutter, and a wider editorial grid at tablet and desktop sizes. Dense image passages alternate with quiet typographic passages. Mobile rails are genuinely horizontal where browsing benefits from continuity; desktop layouts may use asymmetric spans and controlled overlap.

The supplied 4:5 campaign artworks determine image proportions. Crop positions must protect garments and faces, while intact plates must never be squeezed or distorted.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat by default. Depth comes from tonal fields, crop boundaries, thin rules, and occasional layer overlap rather than drop shadows or glass effects.

**The Flat Folio Rule.** A section earns distinction through composition and colour, not a floating white container.

## Shapes

Corners remain square or nearly square. Image masks may use straight-edged editorial crops; controls use restrained geometry and underline or rule-based affordances. Circles, pills, blobs, and oversized rounded rectangles are not part of this world.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** vary section scale and structure while preserving one typographic and spacing grammar.
- **Do** let real garment colours provide strategic saturation.
- **Do** use negative space to frame imagery and strengthen hierarchy.
- **Do** treat mobile crop choices and section heights as primary design decisions.

### Don't:

- **Don't** turn the homepage into a stack of repeated product-card grids.
- **Don't** recreate embedded campaign copy next to the same intact artwork.
- **Don't** add generic commerce UI, fabricated claims, prices, reviews, or store details.
- **Don't** use experimental concept imagery as a production substitute.
