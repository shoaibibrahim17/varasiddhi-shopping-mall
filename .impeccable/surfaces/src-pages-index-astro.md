# Homepage surface

- Scope: `/` homepage for the first production catalogue/brand release.
- Visitor mode: Persuade.
- Audience: people and families in and around Adilabad browsing fashion for everyday and celebration dressing, primarily on mobile.
- Job: understand the breadth and character of Varasiddhi's women, saree, occasion, and men's collections, then regard the Adilabad store as the destination.
- Primary action: explore the catalogue chapters; the visit section establishes intent without inventing an address, hours, or contact channel.
- Proof and content: the approved primary logo and ten supplied catalogue campaign artworks under `src/assets`.
- Constraints: Astro-first static output, only approved local imagery, no fabricated commercial or store facts, and no commerce architecture.
- Chosen direction: **The Celebration Folio** — a lookbook/invitation-folio world with asymmetric chapters, textile colour fields, hairline rules, model-led crops, and intact campaign plates.
- Memorable moment: the first viewport divides into a quiet typographic field, a tightly cropped red saree portrait, and a slim espresso folio rail; on mobile the headline leads directly into the cropped garment.
- Unresolved: approved store imagery, precise street address, contact details, opening hours, and verified social links are not yet available.
